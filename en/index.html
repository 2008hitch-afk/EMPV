03 / FAQ

Questions before we start.

Before adding technology, we define the change that is needed, what should be built and which boundaries should remain explicit.

01What kind of problems do you work on?+

Situations where work depends on manual handoffs, scattered information, disconnected tools or decisions that require people to reconstruct context over and over. Before proposing a solution, we identify where the operational friction starts.

02Do you always start with AI?+

No. AI is an option, not the starting point. If traditional software, integrations or deterministic automation solve the problem better, we prefer the simpler system. We use AI only where it enables something deterministic software handles poorly.

03Do you build everything from scratch?+

Almost never by principle. We first assess what already works, preserve mature specialist tools and build the missing layer around them. We rewrite only what prevents the overall system from working well.

04Can you work with data that needs to stay inside the company?+

Yes. When privacy, data ownership, latency or control justify it, we design local or on-premise architectures and workflows where data does not need to leave the organization. Cloud remains a tool, not a requirement.

05How does a project start?+

We map the process: people, handoffs, tools, data, constraints and decisions. Then we change one of the most repetitive or costly handoffs, measure the result and only then expand the system.

06Can AI Lab projects become startups?+

Some can. The Lab separates an interesting idea from a technical and product thesis that holds up under testing. Once a direction passes technical and product tests, it can evolve into a standalone product, spin-off or new venture.

07Are you open to partnerships or investment?+

For selected directions, yes — especially when the counterpart brings capital, distribution, data, domain expertise or scientific and industrial validation. The site shows the thesis and the problem; details that form part of the operational advantage remain private.
