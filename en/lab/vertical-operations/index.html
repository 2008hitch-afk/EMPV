Product direction / vertical SaaS

Vertical Operations

A product direction for vertical operating systems where what customers buy or book directly generates the work staff needs to perform.

First testThe first complete vertical has been built around pet hospitality.
Vertical SaaSOperationsProduct
Typical vertical software

Modules that coexist

CRM, booking, payments and tasks often exist as separate features that still require operational reconciliation.

Direction

One operational chain

Availability, purchase/booking, service configuration and staff activities share the same domain and generate coherent work.

01 / The thesis

The product should model work, not merely record it.

When a customer buys a service, that fact should automatically generate occupied capacity, tasks, ownership and operational state for the team.

02 / First vertical

Pet resort as a proving ground.

The multi-tenant V1 includes booking engine, customer area, team/roles, facilities and accommodation, tasks, stays, grooming, documents, base payments and operational reporting.

03 / What we are looking for

How much of the operating model is reusable.

The next test is which operational primitives can be reused across other service verticals without turning the product into generic management software.

EMPV / NOTE

The first product is functionally complete at V1 level, while production readiness and expansion into other verticals remain separate phases.
